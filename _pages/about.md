## About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at KAIST, working with Professor Suhyoung Choi. My research concerns three- and four-dimensional topology, particularly the topology of smooth orbifolds.

I am interested in how handle decompositions and surgery descriptions extend from manifolds to orbifolds, and in the geometric and topological phenomena that arise from their singular structures.
