My research lies in low-dimensional topology, with an emphasis on smooth orbifolds and the interplay between local group actions and global topology.

<div class="research-grid">
  <section class="research-card">
    <h2>Orbifold handles and surgery</h2>
    <p>I study handle decompositions of smooth 3- and 4-dimensional orbifolds, including the classification of local quotient handles, their attaching regions, and the resulting surgery operations.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="research-card">
    <h2>Three- and four-dimensional topology</h2>
    <p>I am interested in surgery descriptions, Kirby calculus, smooth structures, and the relationship between orbifold constructions and techniques from manifold topology.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="research-card">
    <h2>Geometric structures</h2>
    <p>My broader interests include real projective structures on Coxeter orbifolds and the geometry of deformation spaces.</p>
  </section>
</div>
